3. DIURNAL DYNAMICS & PHOTOCHEMICAL CYCLE
----------------------------------------
Ground-level ozone exhibits a distinct diurnal cycle governed by solar radiation (driving photolysis of NO2) and nighttime titration (via NO emission).
  * Daytime RMSE (08:00 - 18:00)   : 23.9383 µg/m³
  * Nighttime RMSE (19:00 - 07:00) : 20.9436 µg/m³
  * Most Accurate Hour             : 10:00 (Mean Absolute Error: 0.0856)
  * Least Accurate Hour            : 18:00 (Mean Absolute Error: 11.5031)

Diurnal Phase Analysis:
Error magnitude is relatively consistent across the diurnal cycle, suggesting the architecture balances both daytime production and nighttime destruction dynamics symmetrically.
Observed peak ozone occurs on average at 15:00, while the model predicts the peak at 14:00.
-> WARNING: A phase lag of 1 hours is detected in the diurnal peak.
