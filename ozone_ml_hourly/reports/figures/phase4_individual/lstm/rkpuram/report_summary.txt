Diurnal Phase Analysis:
The model exhibits significantly higher error during daylight hours. This points to a deficiency in modeling the complex, radiation-dependent photochemical generation rate. The model likely underestimates the rapid escalation of ozone during peak solar irradiance.
Observed peak ozone occurs on average at 14:00, while the model predicts the peak at 14:00.
-> Phase alignment is perfect. The model correctly identifies the timing of maximum daily photochemical activity.
