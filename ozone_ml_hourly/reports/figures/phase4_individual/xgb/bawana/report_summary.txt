  * Most Accurate Hour             : 10:00 (Mean Absolute Error: 0.2744)
  * Least Accurate Hour            : 15:00 (Mean Absolute Error: 9.7209)

Diurnal Phase Analysis:
Error magnitude is relatively consistent across the diurnal cycle, suggesting the architecture balances both daytime production and nighttime destruction dynamics symmetrically.
Observed peak ozone occurs on average at 15:00, while the model predicts the peak at 15:00.
-> Phase alignment is perfect. The model correctly identifies the timing of maximum daily photochemical activity.
